How this prototype works

What is real here, what is simulated, and where the line was drawn — so a reviewer does not have to guess.

Genuinely implemented

Runs in this page
  • Weighted-keyword classification with confidence bands and a visible score breakdown.
  • Confidence routing: auto-route ≥ 0.75, route-and-flag 0.45–0.75, hold in triage below 0.45.
  • Sequential ticket IDs in the form REQ-2026-0001.
  • Impact × urgency priority matrix, plus keyword overrides that force P1.
  • Per-priority SLA targets with a live countdown and breach detection.
  • Four-rung escalation ladder evaluated against elapsed time.
  • Full state machine including Awaiting Requester, Awaiting Third Party, Resolved, Reopen.
  • Least-weighted-load assignment across skill groups.
  • Append-only audit trail on every ticket.
  • Analytics computed live from the ticket log.

Simulated or out of scope

Honest limits
  • Channels are a dropdown, not live WhatsApp / SMS / mailbox adapters.
  • Identity comes from a seeded employee list rather than a real HRIS.
  • SLA runs on elapsed hours; production runs on each site's business calendar.
  • LLM intent classification is designed in Part 1 but not called here — the keyword layer is what runs, deliberately, because it is the auditable half.
  • Notifications are shown as audit entries instead of being sent.
  • Storage is this browser only. Every reviewer sees their own copy of the seeded data.
  • Auth and permissions are not modelled; the restricted-queue rule is designed, not enforced.

Try these

A reviewer's two-minute tour
  1. Type "my salary was credited short this month" into the subject — watch Payroll win the score before you finish the sentence.
  2. Type "system not letting me apply leave" — the scores split between HR and IT, confidence falls into the 0.45–0.75 band, and the verdict becomes route-and-flag rather than a silent guess.
  3. Type "need help with the thing from last week" — nothing matches, confidence is 0.00, and it is held in triage for a human. The system refuses to guess.
  4. Set impact to My site or the company and urgency to blocked entirely — priority jumps to P1 and the resolve target collapses to four hours.
  5. Submit anything, then press +24h twice and open the Agent console — the escalation column climbs L0 → L1 → L2 on its own.
  6. Open any ticket, resolve it, then reopen it — the ID stays the same and the reopen counter increments.
